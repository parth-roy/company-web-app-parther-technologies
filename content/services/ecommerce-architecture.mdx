## What is Headless E-Commerce & Marketplace Architecture?

Headless e-commerce architecture is the decoupling of the frontend user presentation layer (the storefront) from the backend commercial logic, product catalog, payment processing, and inventory management systems. By connecting the frontend and backend via high-speed REST and GraphQL APIs, Parther Technologies builds ultra-fast shopping experiences with sub-2.5s Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), dynamic personalized pricing, and complex multi-vendor or reverse-auction marketplace capabilities that monolithic platforms like Shopify or WooCommerce cannot support.

### Technical Architecture & Marketplace Engine

| Module | Architectural Implementation | Performance Benchmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Storefront** | Next.js 15 App Router, React Server Components (RSC) | Sub-2.5s LCP, zero layout shift, and instant mobile navigation |
| **Catalog & Search** | PostgreSQL with `pg_trgm` & Meilisearch / Elasticsearch | Sub-50ms fuzzy full-text product search & dynamic filtering |
| **Payment Gateway** | Razorpay, Stripe, & Cashfree with webhook idempotency | Zero double-charge risk via immutable `ProcessedWebhook` ledgers |
| **Payout & Split Engine** | RazorpayX automated vendor bank payouts & IMPS routing | Instant settlement for marketplace merchants with penny-drop verification |
| **Cart & Session** | Redis IORedis cluster with TTL session hydration | Handling 50,000+ concurrent flash-sale checkout carts |

### Production Marketplace Proof: The GoMyTruck Dual-Mode Engine

Parther Technologies engineered one of the most sophisticated digital commercial marketplaces in Eastern India:
* **Dual-Mode Dispatch:** Instant on-demand booking alongside a real-time **Private Bidding Marketplace** where fleet owners and drivers submit live price bids on bulk consignments.
* **Immutable Bid Audit Trail:** Implemented `BidWindow`, `MarketplaceBid`, and `BidRevision` relational models ensuring every price offer, counter-offer, and negotiation is immutably logged for financial compliance.
* **Double-Entry Wallet System:** Atomic multi-wallet architecture managing customer deposits, driver cash floats, platform commissions, and automated daily bank withdrawals.

### Security, Scalability & Compliance

* **DPDP Act 2023 & PCI-DSS Alignment:** Zero raw card or banking credential storage on application servers; all sensitive tokenization is routed through certified payment gateways.
* **Anti-Fraud Idempotency:** Every financial transaction and order submission is protected by unique client request keys (`X-Payout-Idempotency`), preventing duplicate charges caused by erratic mobile connectivity.

> **Operational SLA:** Guaranteed sub-50ms API catalog search, 99.99% checkout availability, and complete custom ownership with zero percentage-based platform royalties.
